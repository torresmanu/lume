# Lume — claims sheet

What we will and will not say. Engineering must make the first column true.

## We say

- Lume captures smoke at the grate so neighbours are not living through your Sunday.
- Built for charcoal. If it only works on induction, it is not Lume.
- No ducts. No drilling into common structure.
- We reduce visible plume and odour leaving the balcony.
- Quiet enough to talk.
- Filters are sold in cooks, not in fairy-tale months.

## We never say

- It makes charcoal legal where the comunidad or consorcio forbids fire.
- Smokeless. Sin humo. 99% elimination. Military-grade.
- Smart ecosystem. App required.
- Suitable as a kitchen replacement for a ducted rangehood in a closed room.

## Proof the brand must carry

| Proof | How |
|---|---|
| It works on a real parrilla | Side-by-side plume test on coal. Not induction. |
| It belongs on a balcony | Scale: railing, plant, mop. No 4K outdoor kitchen. |
| It is for everyone | Price visible. Gender-neutral hands. No specialist jargon. |
| It is a good neighbour | dB stated calmly. A one-pager a presidente de comunidad can forward. |

## Legal posture

Argentina CCyC art. 1973 and Spain Ley de Propiedad Horizontal art. 7.2 treat smoke as a neighbour injury. Lume is a courtesy device, not a permit.
