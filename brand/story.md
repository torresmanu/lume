# Lume — the story we tell to sell

Campaign name (internal): **The first Sunday**  
Campaign name (public, Neutral Spanish): **No te despidas**

## The scene

You move into an apartment. The patio does not come with you. Everyone already knows the script:

1. Say goodbye to asado.
2. Say hello to angry neighbours, the consorcio WhatsApp, fines.
3. Buy an electric plate and call it a day.

We sell a different first Sunday.

## The three hellos

Do not say goodbye to the fire.  
Do not say hello to the fight.  
Say hello to Lume.  
Domingo de asado — also here.

That is the whole ad. Everything else is proof.

| Beat | Customer feels | We show | We never show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goodbye | Grief. The house, the patio, the Sunday that made them *them*. | Boxes in a landing. An empty balcony. A parrilla still wrapped. | Gaucho farewell. Violin. The old quinta as paradise lost. |
| The wrong hello | Dread. Becoming “the neighbour.” The assembly. The multa. | A closed window. A phone face-down. We do **not** need an angry extra. | Karens, shouting, gavel, red envelopes as comedy. The consorcio as enemy. |
| Hello Lume | Relief that is practical, not magical. | The object over real coal. A thin captured plume. | Unboxing theatre. App. Exploded CGI. |
| Domingo | They invite people. The building is still a building. | Table, hands, late light. A window next door that stays open. | Victory dance. “Problem solved forever.” |

## What we are actually selling

Not a hood. **Permission to keep being the person who lights the fire — without starting the new life as a conflict.**

The product is how. The story is who they get to remain on Sunday.

## Website, in this order

1. **Hero** — Empty new balcony or boxes + the three-beat line. CTA: Comprar Lume / Ver cómo funciona.
2. **The old script** — One column: adiós al fuego. One column: hola al conflicto. No villains. The fear is enough.
3. **The object** — Charcoal. No ducts. No drill. Capture at the grate.
4. **The first Sunday** — People, coal, open window. Not a showroom.
5. **For the building** — Link the consorcio one-pager. Courtesy, not a permit.
6. **Close** — Domingo de asado. También acá.

## Claims the story must not swallow

The story is emotional. The law is not. Never let the ad imply:

- Lume means no fines.
- Lume means the consorcio cannot complain.
- Lume legalises charcoal where it is banned.
- Angry neighbours are fools.

If the reglamento forbids fire, the honest close is: *Lume no habilita el fuego. Hace el fuego más civil, cuando el fuego está permitido.*

The move-in story still works: most people are not banned from *all* cooking on the balcony — they are banned from *being a problem*. We sell not being a problem.

## Tone when we sell

Host, not closer. We are inviting them to Sunday, not saving them from the building. The neighbour stays a person. The consorcio stays a rulebook, not a monster. Lume is the third hello: the only one worth giving in the first week.
