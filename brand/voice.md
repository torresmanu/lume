# Lume — verbal identity

## Name

**Lume.** El Lume. Four letters. LU-me, identical in Madrid and Buenos Aires.

In Galician and Portuguese it means fire. Everywhere else it is a noun the object teaches. Do not explain the etymology on the box.

## Descriptor

Small, never a slogan:

- `Lume — para el fuego`
- `Lume — para el balcón`

Never: extractor, campana, hood, smart, smokeless, sin humo.

## Idea

Fire, without the fight. A civil fire. Charcoal stays. Smoke does not leave the balcony.

## Audience

People who live in buildings and still believe asado is how you gather. Argentina and Spain, first crate.

## Voice

Warm, short, adult. A good host, not a startup.

Use: light, gather, capture, leave, invite, neighbour, balcony, coal.

Never: revolutionize, disrupt, solution, smart ecosystem, pitmaster, 99.9%, military-grade, AI.

## Spanish

| Surface | Rule |
|---|---|
| Object, manual, pack | Infinitive or pictogram. Encender / apagar / limpiar. No vos, no tú. |
| Argentina campaign | Vos is allowed. |
| Spain campaign | Tú. Barbacoa is the everyday act; asado is the ritual. |
| Shared film / site | Neutral Spanish. Vecino is the sacred word. |
