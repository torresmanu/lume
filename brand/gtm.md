# Lume — go to market

Internal. The public campaign stays **No te despidas.**
Canonical URL: `https://lumefuego.com`

We do not sell units until there is a crate, a visible price, and proof (plume on coal, dB, filters in cooks). We sell a Sunday. The job of this phase is names, cities, and whether they already have a parrilla.

## Offer

Not a discount. **Avisame cuando esté listo** — plus a photo they can save, plus the consorcio note they can forward.

## ICP

Primary, first 90 days: 28–45, CABA or primer cordón. Moved from a house or PH, or still owns a parrilla that now lives on a balcony. Hosts. Cares that the window next door stays open. Already pays for charcoal.

Secondary: long-time building dwellers who already got the consorcio message. Gift-givers (parents of the person who just moved).

Spain: the same human, *tú*, *barbacoa* in body copy, *asado* as ritual. Collect on the same form (`country: ES`). Do not run a Spain campaign until the visitor is reading `es-ES`.

Do not target: outdoor-kitchen buyers, pitmasters, smart home, restaurants, people whose reglamento forbids all fire. Honest close: Lume does not enable it.

## Beachhead

Campaign in Argentina first. Spain is the larger housing TAM; Argentina is the hotter ICP. Collect both. First crate is not “Spain because more flats.”

## One loop

Sunday grief or neighbour dread → story site → email + country + optional parrilla → confirmation mail → WhatsApp to a neighbour → consorcio note forward → quiet Sunday notes → first crate offer to the list.

## Channels (90 days)

1. The machine. Capture, confirm, share preview. If these fail, every other channel wastes the click.
2. Neighbour loop. Share `https://lumefuego.com` with `?ref=wa#avisame`. Host voice.
3. Search that already exists. `/preguntas` answers “¿puedo hacer asado en el balcón?” without promising legality. `/consorcio` is the forwardable object.
4. Founder-led, 30 minutes a day. CABA asado groups, building groups, one still per Sunday. One URL only. Never “smokeless.”
5. Earned media, weeks 3–8. Pitch the story TN / La Nación / Fotocasa already cover: the other first Sunday. Send the consorcio PDF, not capture percentages.
6. Paid only after tracking works. Tiny Meta/IG, CABA, 25–45, asado/parrilla. Creative is the three-beat line. Landing is `#avisame`. Default: no ads in the first two weeks.
7. Not yet: inmobiliarias, carbonerías, Mercado Libre, Stripe, influencers who say “sin humo.”

## 90-day numbers

- Week 2: waitlist persists on lumefuego.com; every signup gets mail; OG share works.
- Day 30: 150–300 emails, mostly AR, referral share used, PDF downloads visible.
- Day 90: 800–2,000 emails; know % AR vs ES; know % who already have a parrilla.

## Pitch (journalists)

People move into a building and are told to say goodbye to asado. The other script is a fight with the consorcio. Lume is a charcoal-capable extractor for the balcony parrilla — no ducts, no drilling — so the neighbour’s window can stay open. It is a courtesy, not a permit. If the reglamento forbids fire, Lume does not enable it.
